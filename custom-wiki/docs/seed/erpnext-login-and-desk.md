# Log in to ERPNext Desk

**App:** ERPNext  
**Audience:** end-user  
**Last verified:** 2026-08-07

## Goal
Sign in to the Kecktech ERP desk and reach the home workspace.

## Prerequisites
- Kecktech staff or customer account provisioned in ERPNext
- URL: https://erp.kecktech.net (alias: https://ops.kecktech.net)

## Steps
1. Open https://erp.kecktech.net
2. Complete Authelia SSO if prompted
3. Enter your ERPNext credentials on the login form if asked
4. Confirm you land on Desk (workspace icons visible)

## Verify
You can open **CRM → Customer** or your assigned workspace without an auth error.
