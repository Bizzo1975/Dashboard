# Find an article in the Help Center

**App:** Help Center (custom-wiki)  
**Audience:** end-user  
**Last verified:** 2026-08-07

## Goal
Locate a how-to using search or categories.

## Prerequisites
- Public access (no login required for help.kecktech.net)
- URL: https://help.kecktech.net

## Steps
1. Open https://help.kecktech.net
2. Use the search box for a keyword, or browse categories
3. Open an article and follow the steps

## Verify
Article body loads with steps and related links.
