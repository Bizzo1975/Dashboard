# Sign in to the Customer Portal

**App:** Customer Portal  
**Audience:** end-user  
**Last verified:** 2026-08-07

## Goal
Access the customer portal to view tickets and account status.

## Prerequisites
- Active Kecktech customer account
- URL: https://portal.kecktech.net

## Steps
1. Open https://portal.kecktech.net
2. Sign in with your portal credentials
3. Land on the portal home / dashboard

## Verify
You can see your tickets or account summary without a 401/403.
